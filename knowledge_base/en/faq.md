# Frequently Asked Questions — Himalayan Heritage Hotel

## Booking and Reservations

**Q: How do I make a room reservation?**
A: You can book directly through our AI concierge (this chat), by calling the front desk, or by visiting in person. The AI concierge can check availability, show prices, and confirm your booking in real time.

**Q: Do I need to pay in advance to confirm my booking?**
A: For regular bookings, payment is collected at check-in. For festival periods (Dashain, Tihar, Chhath) and group bookings, a advance deposit of रू 2,000 per room is required to secure the reservation.

**Q: How do I get my Booking ID?**
A: After your booking is confirmed, a Booking ID is sent to your phone number via SMS and also shown in the chat confirmation.

**Q: Can I modify my booking dates?**
A: Yes. Contact our AI concierge or the front desk with your Booking ID and phone number. Date changes are subject to availability.

**Q: What is your cancellation policy?**
A: Full refund if cancelled more than 48 hours before check-in. 50% refund for 24–48 hours before. No refund for less than 24 hours before check-in. Festival bookings have stricter cancellation terms.

## Check-In and Check-Out

**Q: What time is check-in?**
A: Check-in is from 2:00 PM (14:00).

**Q: What time is check-out?**
A: Check-out is by 11:00 AM.

**Q: Can I get a late check-out?**
A: Late check-out until 2:00 PM is available for रू 500 extra, subject to room availability. Please request the evening before or early in the morning on your check-out day.

**Q: What if I arrive before 2 PM?**
A: We can hold your luggage securely. Early check-in may be possible if the room is ready, at no extra charge.

**Q: What ID do I need?**
A: A valid government-issued photo ID — citizenship card (nagarikta), passport, or driving licence.

## Rooms and Amenities

**Q: Does the hotel have WiFi?**
A: Yes. Free high-speed WiFi is available in all rooms and common areas. Ask the front desk for the password.

**Q: Is there AC in every room?**
A: Yes, all room types include air conditioning.

**Q: Do you have a restaurant?**
A: Yes, our restaurant is open 7:00 AM to 9:30 PM and serves Nepali, Indian, and continental dishes. Room service is available until 10:00 PM.

**Q: Do you offer laundry service?**
A: Yes. Laundry handed in before 9:00 AM is returned by 6:00 PM the same day. Cost: रू 400 per load.

## Transport

**Q: Can you pick me up from Biratnagar Airport?**
A: Yes. Airport pickup is available 24/7 for रू 800 per trip. Please book at least 2 hours in advance and provide your flight number and arrival time.

**Q: Can you pick me up from Itahari Bus Park?**
A: Yes. Bus Park pickup is available 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM for रू 300. Book at least 1 hour in advance.

## Tours and Activities

**Q: Do you arrange tours to Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve?**
A: Yes! Our guided day tour costs रू 4,500 per person and includes transport, guide, park entry, and packed lunch. Departure is at 6:00 AM.

**Q: Do you arrange tours to Dharan?**
A: Yes. Our half-day Dharan tour (Bindabasi Temple, Pahadi Bazaar, viewpoint) costs रू 1,200 per person. Departure is at 8:00 AM.

## Payments

**Q: Do you accept credit cards?**
A: No. We accept cash (NPR), eSewa, Khalti, and bank transfer.

**Q: Can I pay with eSewa?**
A: Yes, eSewa is accepted. Scan the QR code at the front desk.

## Contact

**Q: How can I reach the hotel directly?**
A: You can use this AI concierge chat, or ask to be connected to our staff if you need personal assistance.
