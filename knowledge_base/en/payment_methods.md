# Payment Methods — Himalayan Heritage Hotel

## Accepted Payment Methods

### Cash (Nepali Rupees - NPR)
- Accepted at all times for all services
- Change provided for notes up to NPR 1,000
- We do not accept foreign currency directly; please exchange at a bank before arrival

### eSewa
- **How:** Scan our QR code at the front desk, or provide your eSewa mobile number for a push notification
- **eSewa ID:** 9800XXXXXX (displayed at front desk)
- **Service:** Instant confirmation, no additional fee

### Khalti
- **How:** Scan our QR code or use the Khalti app
- **Khalti ID:** Himalayan Heritage Hotel (verified merchant)
- **Service:** Instant confirmation, no additional fee

### Bank Transfer
- **Bank:** Nepal Bank Limited / Sunrise Bank (details provided at check-in)
- **Account name:** Himalayan Heritage Hotel Pvt. Ltd.
- **Use for:** Large advance payments, group bookings, conference hall deposits
- **Note:** Transfer must be confirmed 24 hours before check-in; send screenshot to front desk

## Payment Timeline
- Room bookings: Full payment at check-in (or advance payment to confirm festival/peak season)
- Restaurant: Paid after each meal or added to room bill (settled at check-out)
- Services (tour, transfer, laundry): Paid at time of service or added to room bill

## Invoices and Receipts
- A printed receipt is provided for all payments
- VAT invoices available on request for corporate guests
- Digital receipts can be sent via WhatsApp or email

## Note on Card Payments
- Credit and debit cards are not currently accepted
- An ATM is located at Itahari Chowk (5 minutes walk) and inside Biratnagar Airport
