# Festival and Seasonal Packages — Himalayan Heritage Hotel

## Dashain Package (Bada Dashain)
**Festival period:** October (Ashwin–Kartik, Bikram Sambat calendar)

- **Room rate multiplier:** 1.5x (50% premium on all room types during the main Dashain period)
- **Family Suite special:** 1.75x during peak Dashain (additional premium for the most sought-after accommodation)
- **Package inclusions:** All rooms include complimentary Dashain welcome pack (tikka materials, flowers, sel roti), traditional Dashain dinner on Dashami evening (रू 600 per person), and live Deusi-Bhailo cultural program on selected evenings
- **Availability:** Very limited — we recommend booking 4–6 weeks in advance for Dashain
- **Why stay with us:** Himalayan Heritage Hotel hosts a community Dashain celebration with local dance (Deusi-Bhailo), traditional food stalls, and family-friendly activities

## Tihar Package (Deepawali / Festival of Lights)
**Festival period:** October–November (Kartik)

- **Room rate multiplier:** 1.4x during the main Tihar period
- **Package inclusions:** Decorative marigold garlands on room doors, special Laxmi Puja arrangement, traditional Tihar mithai (sweets) welcome pack, candle-lit restaurant ambiance on Laxmi Puja evening
- **Recommended stay:** 3 nights to fully experience Gai Puja, Laxmi Puja, and Bhai Tika

## Chhath Puja Package
**Festival period:** November (Kartik, 4 days)

- **Room rate multiplier:** 1.3x during Chhath
- **Package inclusions:** Early morning transport (4:30 AM) to Chatara Ghat or local Chhath venue, packed prasad breakfast, return transport
- **Significance:** Chhath is especially important in eastern Nepal (Madhesh region); the hotel caters to families observing the fast

## New Year Package (Naya Barsa / Baisakh 1)
**Festival period:** Mid-April (Baisakh 1, Bikram Sambat new year)

- **Room rate multiplier:** 1.25x
- **Package inclusions:** Special Naya Barsa buffet dinner, cultural program, lucky draw, complimentary breakfast
