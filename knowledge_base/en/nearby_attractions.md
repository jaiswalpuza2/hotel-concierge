# Nearby Attractions and Directions — Himalayan Heritage Hotel

## Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve
- **Distance from hotel:** Approximately 35 km (45–60 minutes by vehicle)
- **Description:** Nepal's smallest wildlife reserve, situated on the flood plains of the Koshi River. Home to wild water buffalo (Arnav), gharial crocodiles, Gangetic dolphins, and over 500 species of birds including rare migratory species. Best visited October–March.
- **Activities:** Bird watching, elephant safari, river rafting, nature walks
- **Entry fee:** NPR 1,000 per person (Nepali nationals NPR 100)
- **Our tour package:** रू 4,500 per person — includes transport, guide, park entry, and packed lunch. Departs 6:00 AM from the hotel.

## Dharan
- **Distance from hotel:** Approximately 18 km from Itahari (30 minutes by vehicle)
- **Key attractions:**
  - **Bindabasi Temple (Budha Subba):** One of eastern Nepal's most sacred Hindu temples on a hilltop. Dedicated to Lord Shiva. Spectacular view of the Terai plains.
  - **Pahadi Bazaar:** Traditional market area with local crafts, Thangka paintings, and street food
  - **Bijayapur Danda:** Viewpoint with panoramic views of the Himalayan foothills
  - **BPKIHS (B.P. Koirala Institute of Health Sciences):** Notable medical institution and botanical garden
- **Our half-day tour package:** रू 1,200 per person — includes transport and guide. Departs 8:00 AM.

## Biratnagar
- **Distance from hotel:** Approximately 25 km (30–40 minutes by vehicle)
- **Key attractions:**
  - Biratnagar Airport (domestic flights to Kathmandu)
  - Rani Pokhari (historic pond)
  - Durga Mandir
  - Industrial town with good shopping for local products
- **Airport pickup/drop service:** Available 24/7. रू 800 per trip.

## Itahari Town (Walking Distance)
- Local market and shops (5 min walk)
- Itahari Chowk (busy intersection, local restaurants)
- Bus Park (connecting to Kathmandu, Biratnagar, Dharan, Jhapa)

## Chatara
- **Distance from hotel:** Approximately 20 km
- **Description:** Sacred Hindu pilgrimage site at the confluence of the Koshi and Saptakoshi rivers. Chatara Ghat is visited by thousands of pilgrims during festivals, especially Chhath Puja.

## Saptakoshi (Koshi River)
- The mighty Koshi River flows nearby. Scenic riverbank walks and fishing spots are accessible within 10 km of the hotel.

- Local auto-rickshaws are inexpensive for Itahari town
- Bus Park (5 min by auto) connects to all major destinations
