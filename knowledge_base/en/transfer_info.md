# Airport and Bus Park Transfer — Himalayan Heritage Hotel

## Biratnagar Airport (BIR) Pickup and Drop
- **Service:** Available 24 hours, 7 days a week
- **Advance booking:** Please book at least 2 hours before your flight/arrival
- **Price:** रू 800 per trip (one-way, for up to 3 passengers and standard luggage)
- **Large group / extra luggage:** रू 1,200 per trip
- **Booking:** Ask the AI concierge, call the front desk, or request at any time
- **What to provide:** Your flight number, arrival/departure time, and number of passengers
- **Distance:** Approximately 25 km, 30–40 minutes depending on traffic
- **Driver will:** Hold a name card at the arrivals gate

## Itahari Bus Park Pickup and Drop
- **Service:** Available 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM
- **Price:** रू 300 per trip (one-way, for up to 3 passengers)
- **Advance booking:** Please book at least 1 hour before your bus arrives
- **What to provide:** Your bus name/company, arrival time, and number of passengers
- **Distance:** Approximately 2 km, 5–10 minutes

## Dharan Bus Park Pickup and Drop
- **Service:** Available 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM
- **Price:** रू 600 per trip (one-way)
- **Distance:** Approximately 18 km, 30–35 minutes

## Biratnagar Bus Park Pickup and Drop
- **Service:** Available 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM
- **Price:** रू 700 per trip (one-way)
- **Distance:** Approximately 23 km, 35–45 minutes

## Notes
- All transfer prices are per vehicle, not per person.
- Transfers must be paid in cash (NPR) or via eSewa/Khalti.
- For international flights connecting through Kathmandu, the hotel can assist with advance booking of Biratnagar–Kathmandu flights.
- Prices may be higher on public holidays and festival days (Dashain, Tihar).
