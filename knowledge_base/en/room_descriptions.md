# Room Descriptions — Himalayan Heritage Hotel

## Standard Room
- **Capacity:** Up to 2 guests
- **Bed type:** Queen bed
- **Base price:** रू 3,500 per night
- **Floor:** Floors 1–4 (20 rooms total: 101–120)
- **Amenities:** Air conditioning, free WiFi, hot water, 32-inch TV, room service, wardrobe, writing desk, en-suite bathroom with shower

## Deluxe Room
- **Capacity:** Up to 2 guests
- **Bed type:** King bed
- **Base price:** रू 5,500 per night
- **Floor:** Floors 2–4 (14 rooms total: 201–214)
- **Amenities:** Air conditioning, free WiFi, hot water, 40-inch Smart TV, minibar, room service, bathtub + shower, mountain view, balcony, premium toiletries, wardrobe, safe

## Family Suite
- **Capacity:** Up to 4 guests
- **Bed type:** 1 king bed + 2 single beds
- **Base price:** रू 9,500 per night
- **Floor:** Floors 3–4 (4 suites total: 301–304)
- **Amenities:** Air conditioning, free WiFi, hot water, 2 TVs (40-inch each), kitchenette with microwave and electric kettle, living room area, bathtub + shower, mountain view, premium toiletries, wardrobe, safe, extra seating area

## Conference Hall
- **Capacity:** Up to 60 persons
- **Base price:** रू 25,000 per day
- **Floor:** Floor 1 (1 hall: C-01)
- **Amenities:** High-definition projector (4K), surround sound system, air conditioning, free WiFi (dedicated high-speed line), whiteboards (2), flip charts, podium with microphone, ceiling-mounted speakers, catering available on request, blackout curtains, rectangular tables (configurable to U-shape or theatre-style)

## Notes
- All rooms include complimentary bottled water (2 bottles per day), daily housekeeping, and welcome tea service.
- Extra beds are available for Standard and Deluxe rooms at रू 800 per night.
- All prices are subject to seasonal multipliers during peak festival periods (Dashain, Tihar, Chhath).
