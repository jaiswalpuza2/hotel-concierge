# Hotel Policies — Himalayan Heritage Hotel

## Check-In and Check-Out
- **Check-in time:** 2:00 PM (14:00). Early check-in is subject to availability; please call ahead.
- **Check-out time:** 11:00 AM (11:00). Late check-out until 2:00 PM can be arranged for an additional charge of रू 500, subject to availability.
- Guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID (citizenship card, passport, or driving licence) at check-in.
- Payment for the full stay is collected at check-in.

## Cancellation Policy
- **More than 48 hours before check-in:** Full refund (100%).
- **24–48 hours before check-in:** 50% refund.
- **Less than 24 hours before check-in:** No refund.
- Group bookings (10 or more guests) require 7 days notice for cancellation with a full refund.
- To cancel a booking, contact the front desk or use our AI concierge. You will need your Booking ID and the phone number used during booking.

## Child Policy
- Children under 5 years of age stay free when sharing a room with parents (no extra bed).
- Children 5–12 years old in an existing bed are charged रू 500 per night.
- An extra bed for a child 5–12 is available for रू 800 per night.

## Pet Policy
- Pets are not permitted inside the hotel premises.

## Smoking Policy
- Smoking is not permitted inside any hotel room or public indoor area.
- A designated smoking area is available in the garden.
- A cleaning fee of रू 2,000 will be charged if smoking is detected in a room.

## Payment Methods
- Cash (Nepali Rupees, NPR)
- eSewa (QR code or mobile transfer)
- Khalti (QR code or mobile transfer)
- Bank transfer (account details provided at check-in)
- Credit/debit cards are not currently accepted.

## ID Requirements
- All adult guests must provide a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in.
- Foreign nationals must present a valid passport.
- Group bookings: one ID per room minimum required.

## Noise and Quiet Hours
- Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.
- Please be considerate of other guests.

## Damage Policy
- Guests are responsible for any damage caused to hotel property during their stay.
- Damage charges will be assessed and added to the final bill.
